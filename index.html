08 — Beyond the Resume

About

I've never built my career around staying inside one function. My background spans business operations, sales, people management, entrepreneurship, international business, client service, project execution, and hands-on operational work. I have launched a small business, helped start production operations, worked with corporate and government clients, supported international business projects, and eventually grew into COO and General Manager responsibility.

That range taught me something I still rely on today: when I enter an unfamiliar problem, I am comfortable learning how the system works before deciding how to fix it. I do not need every problem to fit neatly inside my previous job description.

My academic background in psychology and fine art and design adds another dimension to how I work. Psychology helps me understand people, motivation, conflict, and group dynamics. Design trained me to look at structure, relationships, and how separate elements work together.

I value clear expectations, mutual respect, honesty, and reliability. At the same time, I believe people do their best work when they have enough freedom to think, challenge assumptions, bring ideas forward, and improve the way things are done.

I am most interested in environments where there is real responsibility, strong people, measurable business impact, and room to grow.

Mountains & Caves
Long ascents, high altitude, changing conditions. Mountains teach adaptation, discomfort, and the difference between temporary difficulty and a real limit. On serious ascents — especially as a rope team — people are genuinely responsible for one another. Caving challenges me differently: technical preparation, emotional control, and decision-making under pressure in confined spaces.
Curiosity, Making & Culture
My first degree was in Fine Art and Design. I enjoy photography, drawing, sewing, building and repairing things, and working with plants. The same curiosity draws me to technology, engineering, and science. I love live theater — the combination of acting, writing, costume, music, and staging happening in real time. And when I travel, one of the first things I explore is the local food.
International Background
Born in Ukraine, built a career across multiple industries and contexts, now based in San Jose, CA. That range — across geographies, industries, and types of organizations — is part of how I think and the lens through which I approach new problems.
